## 1. Gold Mine 10+1
**URL:** https://www.bluestone.com/goldmine.html

| Topic | Fact to use |
|---|---|
| What it is | Monthly installment plan for future jewellery purchase |
| Structure | Pay **10** installments → special discount = **1 full installment** on 11th month |
| Min installment | **INR 1,000** (multiples of INR 100) |
| Who can join | 18+, resident Indian |
| Eligible buy | Diamond/gemstone studded, plain gold, preset solitaire |
| Not eligible | Coins, solitaires |
| Early redeem | From **6th** month (after 5 on-time pays): 25% of 1 installment; **8th** month (7 pays): 50% |
| Full benefit | 10 months on time → 100% of 1 installment (+ 1st installment discount where applicable) |
| Late payments | >2 late → no discount |
| Cancel | Voucher for amount paid (no cash refund of installments); no interest paid on installments |
| Blog angles | Wedding saving, festival gifting, “how to buy jewellery in installments”, Diwali/Akshaya Tritiya planning |

**CTA copy pattern:** Plan your purchase with BlueStone Gold Mine → link goldmine.html

## 2. Gold Reserve
**URL:** https://www.bluestone.com/goldreserve.html  
**T&Cs effective:** Nov 17, 2025 (confirm on tnc.html)

| Topic | Fact to use |
|---|---|
| What it is | Monthly plan that **accumulates gold units** at prevailing gold rate |
| Unit | 1 gold unit = 1 gram of **24kt** gold |
| Structure | Pay **10** monthly installments → gold units allocated each payment |
| Special benefit | Pay 10 on time → extra voucher worth up to 1 installment (choose plain-gold 50% **or** non-plain 100%) |
| Redeem | From **2nd month** onwards (subject to T&Cs) |
| Primary voucher use | Diamond/gemstone studded, plain gold, plain platinum, preset solitaire |
| Blog angles | Gold accumulation, gold rate hedge, “save in gold grams”, vs cash saving for jewellery |

**CTA copy pattern:** Lock gold units monthly with Gold Reserve → link goldreserve.html

## 3. Old Gold Exchange / Big Gold Upgrade
**URL:** https://www.bluestone.com/oldgoldexchange.html  
**T&Cs name:** Big Gold Upgrade (in tnc.html)

| Topic | Fact to use |
|---|---|
| What it is | Exchange old **plain** gold for upgrade value toward new BlueStone jewellery |
| Headline offer | Instant **2-karat upgrade** *or* **1% additional value** (customer picks one) |
| Eligible old gold | Plain gold jewellery, biscuits, bars, coins only |
| Not eligible old items | Diamond/gemstone-studded old jewellery |
| KYC | Valid **PAN** mandatory; name must match |
| Karat upgrade | For 17K–<24K old gold → up to 2K upgrade (max 24K) on **studded & preset solitaire** only |
| 1% value upgrade | Available across slabs; for plain-gold purchase path and 14K–<17K / 24K cases |
| Not eligible for upgrade benefits | Coins purchase, Custom Solitaires |
| Voucher life | **7 months**; non-transferable |
| Irreversible | Once purity/value accepted and voucher issued, old gold cannot be returned |
| Blog angles | Melting old jewellery, exchange offers, upgrade karat, wedding refresh |

**CTA copy pattern:** Upgrade your old gold with BlueStone Old Gold Exchange → link oldgoldexchange.html
